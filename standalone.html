Happy Birthday to one of the most precious people in my life!

I don't think words will ever be enough to explain how much you mean to me. Having you as my sister is one of the sweetest gifts life has given me. No matter how much we tease each other, laugh over silly things, or have our little disagreements, there will always be a special place in my heart that belongs only to you.

You are more than my sister. You are a part of my childhood, my memories, my happiness, and my story. Some of my favorite moments are the little things we share, the laughter that comes out of nowhere, and the memories that become more precious as time passes.

On your birthday, I wish you a life filled with genuine happiness, peaceful days, beautiful surprises, good health, and people who love you as much as you deserve. May every dream you hold close to your heart slowly become real.

I may not say it every day, but I am so grateful that I get to call you my sister. No matter where life takes us, you will always be my Didu, someone I will cherish forever.

Keep smiling, keep shining, and never stop believing in the beautiful things waiting for you.

Happy Birthday, my precious Didu! May your world always be filled with pink skies, beautiful dreams, warm hugs, and all the love your heart can hold.

I love you more than this little letter could ever say. 💕🎀

Forever your loving sister. ❤️
